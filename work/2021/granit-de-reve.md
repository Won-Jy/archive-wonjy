description: This is a semi-documentary video tracing my quest for a “dream granite” along the Hérault river, undertaken for an exhibition in Le Vigan in 2021. I followed the river upstream to its source, at the heart of the Aigoual massif; the camera, following me, captured the landscapes along the banks, fragments of debris left by the previous year’s flood, and riverside villages undergoing urbanization. Along the way, I held up each granite stone I collected as a keepsake in front of the iPhone that was filming. Images of these stones, extracted from the video, were used as training data for a GAN algorithm. Having seen only the stones encountered over the course of this journey, the algorithm produces images of granites that do not exist, but that could only have been born of this voyage.
